# Content Planning

## suitable_agents

Marketing

## when_to_use

Use when a campaign, weekly content plan, or topic matrix is needed.

## inputs

- audience
- product or offer
- campaign goal
- approved memories

## outputs

- themes
- post angles
- distribution plan
- CTA suggestions

## workflow

1. Identify the audience and stage.
2. Generate topic clusters and hooks.
3. Match each item to channel and CTA.
4. Note risks, claims, and review points.
